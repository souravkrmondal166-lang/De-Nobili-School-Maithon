About Our School

A Legacy of Jesuit Education

Founded on the principles of St. Ignatius of Loyola, De Nobili School Maithon continues to shape young minds with a holistic approach to education that balances academics, character, and values.

Since Establishment

Jesuit Values

Guided by the motto "Ad Majorem Dei Gloriam" — For the Greater Glory of God — we instill values of integrity, service, and compassion.

Academic Excellence

Affiliated to CISCE, we offer ICSE and ISC programs with a comprehensive curriculum that nurtures critical thinking and creativity.

Holistic Development

Beyond academics, we foster growth through sports, cultural activities, clubs, and community service programs.
